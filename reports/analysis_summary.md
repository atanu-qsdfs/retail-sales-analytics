# Retail Sales Analysis – Summary

## Main Results

- Total revenue: £10,642,110.80
- Total invoices: 19,960
- Unique products: 3,922
- Customers with IDs: 4,338
- Highest revenue country: United Kingdom
- Highest revenue month: November 2011
- Lowest revenue month: February 2011

## Key Insights

1. The United Kingdom generated the highest revenue.
2. November 2011 had the highest monthly revenue.
3. February 2011 had the lowest monthly revenue.
4. Thursday had the highest revenue by weekday.
5. DOTCOM POSTAGE was the top product by revenue.

## Recommendations

1. Monitor popular products to plan stock levels.
2. Study monthly and weekday sales patterns when planning promotions.

## Limitation

The dataset contains sales prices but not product costs. Therefore, revenue should not be treated as profit.
